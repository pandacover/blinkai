# 07 — How optional Clips are enabled

Type: grilling
Status: open

## Question

Clips are in scope but off by default. How does the user turn them on?

Decide among: a single per-Run toggle on the Brief; per-shot opt-in after the Film Plan exists; an advanced settings flag; or another control. Resolution should state the enablement surface and what happens to timing/Assembly when some shots have Clips and others only Stills.
